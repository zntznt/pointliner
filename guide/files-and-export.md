# Files and export

*Part of the [Pointliner guide](README.md). How your work is saved, how to keep a whole folder of
notes, how to tune the display, and how to share an outline with someone who does not have the app.*

---

## Saving your work

You do not need to think about saving: the app stores everything in your browser automatically as you
type.

To keep a file on disk that you own and can back up, press **`Ctrl/Cmd+S`**. Or connect a folder
(see below) and the file updates on disk every time you make a change, with no manual step.

## Working with a folder of notes

Keep a whole library of notes in one folder on your computer and move between them the way you would
tabs: a project note, a daily log, a person page, each its own file but searchable and
[linkable](links-and-references.md#links-across-notes) together.

Connect a folder once (**Chrome or Edge only**) and every note saves to disk as you type and reopens
right where you left it after a reload. From the **File** menu:

- **Switch document** to see all the notes in the folder, jump to one, add a fresh **+ New
  document**, or delete an old one.
- **Disconnect folder** steps back out to single-file mode.

On other browsers the menu shows a copy-link invite to reopen in Chrome or Edge instead. (The deeper
mechanics of the workspace are in the [feature
overview](features.md#linking-and-connecting-notes).)

## Appearance and display

Tune how the outline looks and what it shows: switch to dark mode for night work, pick an accent
color, widen the editing column for a big screen, or reveal completed tasks you would normally keep
out of the way.

- The **theme, color, and width** controls live in the File menu under **Appearance**. Theme cycles
  system, then light, then dark on each click.
- **Show done points** is the checkmark button in the toolbar; turn it on and finished to-dos and
  `DONE` points reappear in the outline.

## Exporting and sharing

Share your outline with someone who does not have the app, post it as Markdown on a blog or in a
README, or create a portable archive. From the **File** menu:

- **Markdown** or **plain text** for posting and sharing.
- **Self-contained HTML** packs the entire app and your document into one file. It works offline,
  opens in any browser, and keeps all the interactive features (dice, generators, and calculations)
  live, so the person you hand it to can re-roll and recompute on their own machine.
